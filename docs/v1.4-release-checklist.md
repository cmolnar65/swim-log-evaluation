# v1.4 release checklist

## Scope

Release 1.4 is based on the authorized develop implementation. Database schema remains 1.7; no new migration was added for single-workout goals or the version update. The additive schema versions through 1.7 preserve existing records.

Highlights include:

- Owner-private Health Connect batch imports and one-time owner tokens.
- Private Garmin measurement imports, historical jobs and sleep/SpO2 presentation.
- Administrator-authorized iHealth measurement imports and duplicate review.
- Separate running and walking history and totals, excluded from swimming analytics.
- Canonical read-only workout visibility and independently gated route sharing.
- Per-swimmer units, preserved meter/yard course separation, and swimming goals including single-workout targets and frozen completion progress.
- Explicit unlinking of planned and structured workouts with recorded history preserved.

## Known coverage limits

This release does not claim complete live Garmin subtype-key coverage or physical-device acceptance. Garmin account availability is provider/account dependent. Health Connect support is bounded by the supported receiver contract in REST-API.md. No private workout files, routes, PDFs, credentials, tokens, personal payloads or extracted personal data may enter Git, Actions inputs/logs or packages.
