# Private swimming goals — v1.3 development

Goals are private, owner-scoped records. The **Swim Training → Goals** screen has **Set Goal** and **List Goals** sections and shows live progress/status for each saved goal. Goals do not change recorded workouts, personal-best records, planned workouts, public workout visibility, or coaching/evaluation totals.

## One-time distance and pace

Set a supported evaluated swim distance, meter or yard course, target pace per 100 native course units, and a **Do it by** date. A goal is achieved when a recorded evaluated performance at that exact distance and course meets or beats the pace target by the inclusive due date. Any stroke can qualify. The goal begins on its creation date; earlier performances do not complete a newly created goal. Distances in the other course do not qualify. Missing or unevaluated timing is not inferred.

## Date-range swimming distance

Set a total distance and inclusive first/last dates. All recorded pool and open-water swimming within that period contributes, including swimming with any stroke. Land activities and missing swim distances do not. Stored workout distance remains canonical meters; a yard target is normalized once for progress comparison, then shown in the goal's selected unit. Individual meter and yard workouts, personal-best events and course-specific analytics remain separate.

Progress and completion dates are recalculated from the owner's current recorded workout history. For a date-range goal, the completion date is the date of the first recorded swim that brings the cumulative total to the target; progress then freezes at the target and later swims in the range do not increase it. For a one-time pace goal, the completion date is the first qualifying performance date. The status is Upcoming, In progress, Achieved or Not achieved by date. Reimporting, correcting or deleting a workout may therefore change the displayed progress or completion date without rewriting the goal. There is no predicted result, readiness score, or automatic training prescription.

## Calendar and privacy

The private authenticated calendar shows a one-time goal on its due date and a date-range goal on its first and last day. A one-day date-range goal has one combined start/end marker. Calendar goal details show the goal target, current progress and status. The calendar REST response is owner-authorized and marked private/no-store; goals are not exposed by public workout views or the external data-portability API.

Goal creation/deletion and cross-swimmer administration use existing workout-management capability checks, signed swimmer context and a nonce. Goal lookups, calculations and deletion include the swimmer ID.

## Storage migration

Schema 1.6 adds an indexed goals table with no backfill. Installation/upgrade uses the existing idempotent `dbDelta` path and verifies the required columns/index before advancing the schema version. Existing workout, performance and other historical rows are not rewritten or removed. The migration is safe to retry after partial table creation.
